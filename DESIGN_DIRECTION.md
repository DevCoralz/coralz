# Coralz — Art Direction

## Goal
Premium independent-studio portfolio: restrained typography, cinematic motion, strong editorial spacing, and interaction that rewards exploration.

## Interaction hierarchy
1. Ambient: hero video, orbital rings, subtle cursor aura.
2. Responsive: pointer parallax and magnetic controls.
3. Scroll: progress indicator, reveal timing, vertical drift.
4. Hover: project light sweep, service arrow movement, lift/elevation.
5. Accessibility: reduced-motion disables decorative animation.

## Avoid
- Oversized display typography across every section.
- Generic gradient blobs as the primary visual language.
- Excessive card borders.
- Animation that blocks reading or navigation.
- Copying another portfolio's layout or identity literally.
